A free online calculator for estimating **DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) landed cost** on cross-border ocean and air freight. Enter your destination, shipping method, carton count, package dimensions, declared value, and tariff tier — the tool instantly returns the total DDP shipping cost, billable weight, volumetric weight, CBM, and a full cost breakdown including freight, customs duty, and DDP service fee.

**?? Open the calculator: [https://factory-oem.com/Ai/DDP/](https://factory-oem.com/Ai/DDP/)**

> **Estimates only.** All freight rates, duty tiers, and service fees are simplified approximations for planning and educational purposes. Actual charges depend on your forwarder's quote and destination customs rules. Always confirm with your logistics provider before shipping.

## How to Use

1. Open **[https://factory-oem.com/Ai/DDP/](https://factory-oem.com/Ai/DDP/)** in any modern browser.
2. Select a **destination region** — United States, European Union, United Kingdom, or Canada.
3. Choose a **shipping method** — Sea Freight (DDP Economy) or Air Freight (DDP Fast).
4. Enter the **carton count** and **gross weight per carton** (kg).
5. Enter the **package dimensions** per box (L × W × H in cm).
6. Enter the **total declared value** in USD and select a **tariff tier**.
7. Read the results panel: total estimated DDP cost, chargeable weight, volumetric weight, CBM, and itemised cost breakdown.

No account, sign-up, or installation is required. The tool runs entirely in your browser.

---

## Features

- **Destination-aware pricing** — separate rate tables for the US, EU, UK, and Canada.
- **Ocean vs. air comparison** — switch between sea freight (per CBM, with minimum charge) and air freight (per kg, with minimum charge) in one click.
- **Dimensional weight aware** — volumetric weight is calculated using the air-freight divisor (`L×W×H(cm) / 6000`) and the sea-freight conversion (`1 CBM ≈ 167 kg`); chargeable weight is the greater of actual and volumetric weight.
- **Full DDP cost breakdown** — freight, customs duty & tax, and DDP service fee are shown as separate line items, plus a grand total.
- **Tariff tier selector** — choose from common duty bands (5% household goods, 8% consumer electronics, 12% textiles, 25% Section 301 items).
- **Total volume (CBM)** — displayed automatically for ocean-freight planning and container loading.
- **No installation** — works in any modern desktop or mobile browser.
